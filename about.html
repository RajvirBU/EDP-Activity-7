What We Offer
Core Features

Our information system manages every aspect of the Net & Brew experience — from inventory to customer loyalty.

📊
Real-time Dashboard

Monitor sales, stock levels, and staff performance from a unified command center. Decisions powered by live data.

🛒
Sales & POS Tracking

Capture every transaction with linked customer and employee records. Full sale item breakdown with unit pricing.

📦
Inventory Management

Automatic stock deduction on sale, restock via stored procedures, and low-stock alerts powered by database views.

🎁
Loyalty Points System

Reward your most loyal gamers and coffee lovers. Track customer spend and loyalty points across all visits.

👥
Staff Performance

View_staff_performance tracks each barista's sales count and total revenue — perfect for performance reviews.

📈
Report Generator

Generate daily sales reports, low-stock alerts, and customer spend analysis with one-click exports.
